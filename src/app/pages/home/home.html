How to read it

  • Ease compares each team's fixtures in the range with the league average, which is 100. A score of 120 means fixtures 20% easier than average. Double gameweeks count twice and blank gameweeks count as nothing.
  • Colours run from red (hardest) to green (easiest), judged on the opponent and venue only, so a strong team's easy fixture looks as easy as anyone's.
  • Projected goals and clean sheets are for the team itself, and do include how good it is.
